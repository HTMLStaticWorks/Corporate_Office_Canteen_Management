The MealDesk Story

Started in 2024 by a group of ex-corporate employees who were tired of long canteen queues, unorganized payments, and limited lunch choices. We realized that while office technology evolved rapidly, the cafeteria experience remained stuck in the past.

We built MealDesk to connect employees, catering staff, and office administrators onto a single, transparent digital platform. Today, we process over 50,000 meals daily across 200 corporate campuses.
