How MealDesk Works

From logging in to enjoying your hot lunch, explore the seamless journey designed specifically for corporate employees.

The Ordering Journey

1. Login & Connect

Use your corporate SSO or registered work email to log securely into your personalized MealDesk employee dashboard.

2. Browse Daily Menus

Explore today's specific selections. Filter by vegetarian, vegan, or low-calorie options to find exactly what fits your diet.

3. Pre-Order & Schedule

Select your meal, set your preferred pickup time (e.g., 1:00 PM), and add it to your order queue before the kitchen cutoff time.

Cashless Wallet System

Say goodbye to fumbling for cash or swiping cards at the counter. The MealDesk wallet simplifies everything.

Easy Top-Ups

Add funds using credit card, debit card, or UPI instantly.

Corporate Subsidies

Your company can automatically deposit monthly food allowances directly into your digital wallet.

1-Click Checkout

Confirm your meal instantly. The exact amount is deducted from your balance securely.

Smart Pickup Process
