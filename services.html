Digital Pre-Ordering

Empower your employees to order their lunch straight from their desks or during their commute. No more waiting in long lines during the peak lunch hour.

  • Browse daily rotating menus with nutritional information.

Closed-Loop Wallet System

Eliminate cash handling completely. Our secure digital wallet allows employees to top-up funds or connect directly to corporate payroll subsidies.
