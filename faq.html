Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about ordering, payments, and managing your corporate meal account.

Ordering & Menus

You can pre-order your meals up to one week in advance. The daily menu is usually published by Friday for the following week, allowing you to plan your lunches ahead of time.

Yes. For same-day lunch pickup, orders must be placed before 10:30 AM. This gives our kitchen staff exact numbers to minimize food waste and ensure freshness.

Absolutely. On the Menu page and within your Dashboard, you can filter meals by Vegetarian, Vegan, Gluten-Free, and specific allergens. Each meal card also displays comprehensive ingredient lists.

Wallet & Payments

Navigate to the Wallet section in your dashboard. You can add funds securely using your Credit Card, Debit Card, or UPI. There are no transaction fees for wallet top-ups.

If your employer provides a meal subsidy, it will automatically reflect in your wallet balance on the 1st of every month. You can use these funds just like regular topped-up money.

Yes. If you are leaving the company, you can request a full refund of your self-added funds by contacting support. Note that corporate subsidized funds usually cannot be refunded as cash.

Pickup & Cancellation

When your order is ready, you will receive a notification and a QR code in your dashboard. Simply go to the designated pickup counter and scan your QR code to collect your meal.

We hold meals in warming stations for up to 30 minutes past your scheduled pickup time. After that, we cannot guarantee the temperature or freshness of the meal.

Yes, you can cancel an order directly from the "My Orders" tab in your dashboard, provided you do so before the 10:30 AM kitchen cutoff time. Funds will be instantly refunded to your wallet.
